The path

How a job moves

  1. 01

    The shop adds a scan, or a customer sends an STL or OBJ.

  2. 02

    The part opens on a phone. They turn it and check the shape.

  3. 03

    The shop sets a price on the desk. That number shows on the same job.

  4. 04

    The customer approves it here. The printer stays in the shop.
